# Saved mechanics data

All CSV floating-point values are written with full double precision; SI units are indicated by column suffixes. A bar is 100000 Pa. Null JSON values and CSV NaNs mean undefined/unavailable, never zero.

- `trajectories/{RP,KM}_X.XXXbar.csv.gz`: `t_s` (s), `R_m` (m), `U_m_s` (m/s), `p_infty_Pa` (absolute liquid far-field Pa), `pg_Pa` (noncondensable gas partial pressure Pa), `D_m` (m), `wall_Mach` (dimensionless, abs(U)/1482). For RP, D is only the KM expression evaluated on the RP state; RP does not use it dynamically.
- `*_accepted.npz`: `t_s`, `R_m`, `U_m_s`, all accepted nodes, plus a located termination point if a numerical guard is reached. These arrays do not by themselves contain the integrator's dense polynomials.
- `*.json`: initial physical parameters, numerical settings, run summary and every radius/velocity event. `summary.completed=false` means that the requested five-cycle solution is missing its tail. Last accepted time/state, solver status/reason and censoring are explicit. No missing tail is fabricated.
- Dense export: union of uniform 5001 samples, four subdivisions of every accepted interval, all localized extrema, 65 samples per minimum bracket, and 900 logarithmic offsets on each side of the deepest minimum. The latter span local floating-point time spacing to 2 microseconds. Values are evaluations of the computed dense polynomials. Subsequent mechanics users need not solve again; they must quantify any further interpolation error from the retained sample spacing. No later thermodynamic calculation is included.
- `amplitude_summary.csv`: global radius/speed extrema include endpoints of the observed interval. They are global over five cycles ONLY where `completed=True` and `extrema_scope=global_on_requested_interval`. Else they are censored observed-interval bounds, not physical minima. `Umax_positive_m_s` and `Umax_inward_m_s` are nonnegative magnitudes. Maximum wall Mach uses the physical reference c even for RP. The global volume ratio cubes Rmax/Rmin; it can use different cycles. `paired_*` quantities use the immediately preceding local maximum of the deepest local minimum.
- The characteristic collapse is the deepest post-initial LOCAL minimum. Its duration is measured from its immediately preceding local maximum. The first strong collapse is the first minimum with R<R0/2. This is only an event-selection convention and does not set physical applicability. `characteristic_minimum_cycle` is floor(t/T)+1. Pa=0 has no counted physical local events.
- `extrema.csv`: every post-initial radius/velocity zero-crossing extremum, its time, radius and speed. The root-time request is 1e-24 s; actual double-precision time resolution and ODE error are larger. Numerical convergence is evaluated independently.
- `comparison.csv`: signed KM-minus-RP and relative signed differences divided by abs(RP), for COMPLETE five-cycle metrics only. Source RP/KM values remain in their columns; completed flags must be read before interpreting them. Undefined relative differences at zero are NaN.
- `cycle_comparison.csv`: for each complete acoustic cycle, the deepest local minimum is selected independently for RP and KM, together with its own preceding local maximum and paired duration. A cycle can contain multiple rebounds and collapses; the two selected minima need not represent the same physical event, even though they belong to the same acoustic cycle. `KM_minus_RP_time_s` is the KM selected-minimum time minus the RP selected-minimum time, in seconds; it describes the difference between these independent selections and must not be interpreted as a delay of one physically corresponding event without additional event matching. For comparison of the first strong collapses under the shared rule R<R0/2, use `first_strong_time_s` in `amplitude_summary.csv` (or the per-run JSON summaries), not the cycle-deepest times. Global deepest minima over five cycles form a third selection and may belong to different cycles. Incomplete cycles are unavailable; earlier complete cycles of an incomplete run remain available as descriptive cycle summaries.
- `preliminary_scan.csv`, `scan_design.json`: preliminary amplitudes, observed outcomes and resulting refinement intervals. Log-radius slopes guide a computational mesh; they are not a bifurcation analysis.
- `convergence.csv`: separate amplitude/model reference (tight DOP853) for each relative error; full settings and completion status are retained. Speed/radius root localization is independent of plot sampling.
- `c_limit.csv`, `c_limit_extended.csv`: mathematical sound-speed variation with the gas/liquid constants otherwise fixed. Error grids are the union of event-aware trajectories. Common-time errors include collapse phase shifts. These unphysical sound speeds are not alternative water properties.
- `parameter_provenance.json`: physical meaning, values, units, source and assumption/property status; none are measured in this study.
- Pressure-change / bulk-modulus diagnostic: maximum over the saved dense export of abs(pL-p0)/(rho*c^2). This is a sampled warning proxy, not an EOS-predicted density change. The gas-pressure maximum is evaluated at the refined minimum radius.

`admission.csv` records the permissible next-stage use of each trajectory. A complete and converged mechanical solution remains conditional on its gas and liquid assumptions; no trajectory is an experimentally validated physical collapse prediction here.
